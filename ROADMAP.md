# Deferred experiments

1. Prototype 80% Eurekas and persistent, capped contributions from several discoveries. Include encounters with advanced military units and prevent repeated farming. Establish AI progress before reducing early passive science.
2. Move Campuses only after testing early progression, building prerequisites, unique districts, Great Scientists and technology objectives. Industrial-era unlocks require a separate balance design.
3. Prototype movable herds, capture, controlled breeding and placement while retaining strategic horses for cavalry and AI. Mobile cities and replacing strategic horses follow only after an AI-capable prototype.
